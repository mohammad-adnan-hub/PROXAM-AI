Frequently Asked Questions

Do students have to download any extensions or apps? expand_more

Never. Proxam AI executes entirely inside standard HTML5 WebAPIs (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). There are zero executables, background drivers, or plugins to download.

Is student room video recorded or stored in the cloud? expand_more

No raw webcam footage leaves the client browser. Light on-device neural nets verify user presence and tab focus in real time. Only structured telemetry timestamps are archived.

What happens if a student's internet cuts out mid-test? expand_more

Every MCQ selection is persistently cached to local client storage. When connectivity resumes, answers synchronize invisibly without invalidating the exam attempt.
