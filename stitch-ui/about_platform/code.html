The Systemic Breakdown

Traditional proctoring software treated every student as a suspected adversary.

For a decade, institutions relied on invasive background processes that gained root access to student laptops, logged keystrokes, and uploaded hours of uncompressed bedroom video into centralized third-party servers. The result was rampant test anxiety, false positives driven by biased models, and immense legal liability. Proxam AI was built by computational ethicists and educators who recognized that true academic honesty does not require total surveillance.
